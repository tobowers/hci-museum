## Subagent results (parallel, 2026-10-25)

### Angle 1 — Capacitive touch-through-glass audio (STRONG)
- **B&O Beocenter 9500 (1989-94)**: integrated AM/FM/CD/cassette, mirror-polished aluminium + smoked glass, NO mechanical buttons. Sensi-touch condenser control: graphite printed on underside of glass, charged with HF current; finger PROXIMITY invokes control. Only relevant LEDs lit. CD/cassette lids glide open on light touch of display. Designer David Lewis (completing Jacob Jensen's Beocenter 9000 design), iF Product Design Award 1990. Image verified: **File:BeoCenter9500.JPG, CC BY-SA 3.0** (already downloaded). Distinct: zero-force proximity touch audio control. NOT in collection.
- Lineage note: B&O's touch lineage began with Beomaster 1900 (1976), but 9500 is the full glass-panel culmination.

## What was added
**National Easa-Phone Answering Machine (c. 1979)** — Matsushita (National/Panasonic) consumer answerphone with two endless-loop cassette tapes (one greeting, one message bank). Because each tape is a continuous ring that physically cannot be rewound, replaying a message means waiting for the loop to come around, tracked by a mechanical counter. The storage medium's geometry shapes the interaction — a literal case of "the medium IS the interface." Belongs to the museum's medium-as-interface family (2-XL, Sony Typecorder, IBM 6:5). 2 CC0 Commons images.

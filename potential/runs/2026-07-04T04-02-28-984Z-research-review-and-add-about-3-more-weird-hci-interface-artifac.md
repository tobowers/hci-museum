# Agent Scout Trace

Topic: research, review, and add about 3 more weird HCI interface artifacts from 1976-1992

## Phase 1: Parallel Research (5 subagents launched)

1. **Bizarre input devices** — returned Electronic Nose (1982), Exos Hand Master (1987), The Hands (1984), LJN Roll & Rocker (1989), Super Chair (~1990), Mind Mirror (1982), The Mole (1984), Bandai Terebikko (1988), Buchla Thunder (1989), Sega Activator (1992-93). Most were either already deferred or too obscure.

2. **SynthAxe deep research** — Excellent results. Three co-inventors (Aitken, Dixon, Sedivy) + David Fowler. US Patent 4,658,690. Virgin Group funding. <100 units, £10K. Decoupled pitch/trigger architecture. Detailed interaction mechanics documented. Good source/image availability. **STRONG CANDIDATE**.

3. **Olfactory interfaces** — Dead end for 1976-1992 era. Computer-controlled olfactometers existed in labs but no HCI-intentional scent displays before ~1993. Noted for memory.

4. **Whole-body interfaces** — Returned Atari Puffer (1982-83, cancelled), Autodesk HighCycle (late 1980s, VR bike), Autodesk Virtual Racquetball, CompuTrainer (1986), Foot Craz (1987), Concept II eRow, MIDI Dancer (~1989-92), DIEM Digital Dance (1990), NeuroCom EquiTest (1985-87), NeuroCom Balance Master. CompuTrainer most interesting but borderline museum fit.

5. **Assistive tech** — Returned Adaptive Firmware Card, Touch/Light Talker, Canon Communicator, HandiVoice HC 120, Darci, BAT Keyboard, Unicorn Board, Ke:nx, Lightwriter, LifeSuit, Braille 'n Speak. Most were incremental on existing paradigms or software-driven. HandiVoice (1978-79) was most novel but documentation/image concerns.

## Phase 2: Deeper Verification

6. **Roll & Rocker verification** — Confirmed REAL but EXTREMELY obscure. Announced by LJN 1989, mentioned in Popular Mechanics Oct 1989. One known product photo (Houston Press archive). No confirmed surviving units. No collector documentation. **REJECTED — too poorly documented**.

7. **Additional weird candidates** — Subagent returned Roll & Rocker (again), Yamaha BC Breath Controller (deferred), Buchla Thunder (deferred). No fresh candidates.

8. **SynthAxe images** — Verified 4 good images downloadable. Wikimedia Commons (CC license, 47KB), John Hollis full view (105KB), US Patent drawing (60KB, public domain), Allan Holdsworth live (508KB).

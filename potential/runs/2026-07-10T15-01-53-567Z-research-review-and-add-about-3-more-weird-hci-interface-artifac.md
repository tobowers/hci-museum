# Agent Scout Trace

Topic: research, review, and add about 3 more weird HCI interface artifacts from 1976-1992

## Subagent Round 1 (parallel)

### Consumer HCI subagent found:
- Atari Video Music C-240 (1977) — audio→visual synthesis with knobs. Interesting concept but interaction model (knob-twiddling) isn't novel hardware HCI.
- Coleco Telstar Arcade (1977) — triangular console, each side different controller. Form-is-function. But individual controllers (steering wheel, light gun, paddles) are standard.
- Miracle Piano Teaching System (1990) — ALREADY DEFERRED (memory line 93).
- Spaceball 2003 (c. 1992) — ALREADY DEFERRED (memory line 82, overlaps with DLR Control Ball).
- Simmons SDS-V (1981) — e-drum kit. Standard drum pads. Music section already well-covered.

### Research lab subagent found:
- Rutgers Portable Dextrous Master (1992) — ALREADY DEFERRED (memory line 78).
- **Grimes Digital Data Entry Glove (1983)** — Bell Labs. Finger-to-finger contact detection as chorded keyboard worn as glove. Distinct from all existing chorded keyboards (use physical switches) and all gesture gloves (track position, not contact). Patent US4414537A. PROMISING.
- **Graphical Marionette (1976)** — MIT Architecture Machine Group. Instrumented puppet as real-time 3D animation input device. Predates tangible UI by 20 years. SIGGRAPH 1976 paper, MIT DSpace thesis. PROMISING.

### Arcade subagent found:
- **Taito Sonic Blast Man (1990)** — Player wears padded gloves and physically punches motorized target pad. Force measurement as game input. CPSC fined Taito $50K for injuries. PROMISING.
- Sega Time Traveler (1991) — Concave mirror holographic illusion display. Novel display, but input is standard joystick.
- Bally Sente Stompin' (1986) — Foot-pad stomping. Overlaps with NES Power Pad (1988) and Amiga Joyboard (1983) already in museum.
- Galaxian³ (1990) — 28-player hydraulic motion platform + 360° projection. Impressive but controller is just targeting cursor; R360 already covers motion platform.
- Taito Darius (1987) — Triple-monitor mirror-blended panorama + body sonic seat. Display + haptic innovation, but input is standard joystick.

### Medical/accessibility subagent found:
- Optacon (1971-96) — Handheld camera → fingertip tactile pin array. Truly pioneering sensory substitution. But first manufactured 1971 (before era window), though sold through 1996. Worth considering.
- Sonicguide MKII (1975) — Ultrasonic glasses → binaural audio encoding. 1975 is just pre-era. Interesting auditory substitution.
- Mowat Sensor (1977) — Handheld sonar → vibration. Simple distance→vib mapping. Minimal HCI novelty.
- Tickle Talker (1985) — Electrotactile speech aid. Interesting but research prototype only, thin on images.
- Kin-Com 500H (1982) — Motorized dynamometer with force curve biofeedback. More medical instrument than HCI artifact.

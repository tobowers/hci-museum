## Verification Round

### Technos Acxel (1987)
- **Status**: VERIFIED. 2,114-LED conductive touch matrix confirmed by multiple sources. Pierre Guilmette (inventor) and Nil Parent (design) confirmed. ~39 units delivered 1989-1992. £8,500-£37,930 price range.
- **Images**: Accessible (HTTP 200) from Idarca (inventor's company site) and magazine scans (mu:zines). NO freely-licensed images. Idarca images are copyrighted. No institutional museum holds one.
- **Decision**: DEFERRED — strong HCI story, genuinely novel interaction model, but image-blocked. Would need permission from Pierre Guilmette/Idarca or patent drawing extraction. info.json built at potential/technos-acxel/.

### Tickle Talker (1985-87)
- **Status**: VERIFIED. Electrotactile (not vibrotactile) confirmed by multiple PubMed-indexed papers. Team confirmed (Blamey, Cowan, Galvin, Clark). Clinical trials, never commercialized.
- **Images**: Embedded in SST-90 PDF (needs pdfimages extraction). Academic paper figures behind paywalls. No museum holdings confirmed. No freely-licensed images found.
- **Decision**: DEFERRED — genuine modality gap (electrocutaneous vs vibrotactile), but image-blocked. Would need PDF image extraction or academic paper figure permissions. info.json built at potential/tickle-talker/.

### Etch A Sketch Animator 2000 (1987)
- **Status**: VERIFIED from Wikipedia. Has Wikimedia Commons image (CC-licensed). "Magic Touchpad" in 1987 predates laptop touchpads.
- **Decision**: NOT PURSUED. Interaction model (touchpad drawing on LCD) is incremental — the museum already has KoalaPad (1984) for touchpad input. The device is a standalone drawing toy, not a novel HCI paradigm. No info.json built.

### LJN Video Art (1987)
- **Status**: VERIFIED from Wikipedia. Dedicated TV drawing console, joystick-based drawing, VCR animation recording. Panned by critics (Roger Ebert: "results look crummy").
- **Decision**: NOT PURSUED. Interaction model (joystick for drawing) is using the most common input device for an inappropriate task — an interesting HCI failure story but not a novel interaction paradigm. No info.json built.

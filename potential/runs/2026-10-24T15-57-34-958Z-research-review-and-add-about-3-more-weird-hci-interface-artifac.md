### Angle 2 — Home audio/entertainment control interfaces  (STRONG)
- **B&O Beocenter 9500 (1989-93)**: FULL capacitive touch-sensing through glass panel, NO mechanical buttons. Printed graphite circuit under smoked glass, finger proximity changes capacitance. Only relevant-mode LEDs lit. CD/cassette lids glide open on touch. ~£1,695. Jacob Jensen/David Lewis design. **Commons CC BY-SA image confirmed: File:BeoCenter9500.JPG**. Distinct interaction: zero-force touch audio control.
- **Sony CDK-008 "Discojockey" (1985-86)**: 60-disc carousel CD changer with keypad+LCD programming interface AND RS-232 serial port for computer control (C64/PC). No Commons image.
- **B&O Beomaster 8000 (1982)**: capacitive touch front panel + capacitive sliding volume strip (no moving parts), wired touch remote. No Commons image for specific model.

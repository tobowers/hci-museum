## Subagent results (3 parallel)

### S1 — Theatrical lighting consoles: Strand Light Palette (1978), Strand Century, designer David Cunningham
STRONG HCI story: first CRT + command-line lighting console; lighting designers typed sentences like "CH 1 @ 5" on a dedicated keypad; command-line/tracking philosophy still industry standard (ETC Eos). Installed Goodspeed Opera House; Broadway premiere Beatlemania; 300+ family systems by 1987; LP90 sold 700+. Period docs on Theatrecrafts (1978 brochure, Feb 1980 operator manual). BLOCKER: zero CC images on Commons; best photos are Theatrecrafts Backstage Heritage Collection (copyright unclear) — direct URLs: theatrecrafts.com/archive/documents/photo_light_palette_1978.jpg, lightpalette1_01..05.jpg. Need to assess licensing. Correct attribution: David Cunningham, NOT Gordon Pearlman.

### S2 — Music-keyboard computers: Yamaha CX5M (1984), Yamaha Corp
STRONG. MSX computer with built-in 8-voice FM synth (YM2151/SFG-01), MIDI, sold in music stores. Composition via QWERTY typing into stave editor (YRM-101 FM Music Composer) and BASIC music macros (YRM-104: `_PHRASE(1,"cdefg")`) — CALL MUSIC wakes the synth. "One of only two MSX machines sold in the US." DX7 voicing cartridge (YRM-103) story. ~£1,000 UK 1985. Cartridge ROMs preserved/emulated (cx5m.net). Three CC images on Commons incl. MIM Brussels (CC BY-SA 2.0) and Personal Computer Museum Brantford (CC BY 2.0, Marcin Wichary) + SFG-01 board shot (CC BY-SA 3.0). RECOMMEND PROMOTE. Note: 2026-07-08 scout judged it "too standard" — but as instrument-as-computer-appliance category representative it's canonical.

### S3 — Computerized domestic sewing machines: Pfaff Creative 1471 (1984/85-86), G.M. Pfaff AG Kaiserslautern
STRONG concept, fills "domestic embedded computer" gap. Pfaff official history: 1979 first programmable machine; 1984 first computerized with display + user-programmable stitches (Creative 1471 line); 1988 Designer. ~$1,000-1,400 at launch; 99+ stitches; LCD readout; stitch-count buttonholes (quirk documented by owners); IDT feed. Successors: 1473, 1475CD (1991). Competition: Bernina 1130 (1986), Husqvarna Viking Computer series, Janome Memory Craft. BLOCKER: no free image of the 1471 itself. Available CC images: Bernina 1130 official photo (CC BY-SA 3.0), Pfaff select 1538 (later Pfaff, CC BY-SA 3.0), Husqvarna Viking shots. Precedent exists for representative-image + honest captioning (Lightwriter SL1/SL35, Kay Visi-Pitch).

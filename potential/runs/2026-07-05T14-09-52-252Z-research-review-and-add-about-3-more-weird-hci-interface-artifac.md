# Agent Scout Trace

Topic: research, review, and add about 3 more weird HCI interface artifacts from 1976-1992

## Phase 1: Parallel research (5 subagents)

### Physical computing subagent
- The Robotic Workshop (1987) - Capsela + C64, Mindstorms predecessor
- Fischertechnik Interface (1984-85) - German industrial construction + computer
- BBC Buggy (1983) - Educational robot for BBC Micro

### Weird commercial failures subagent
- Mattel Intellivoice (1982) - Speech synthesis for Intellivision
- Bandai Terebikko (1988) - Interactive VHS telephone
- VTech Socrates (1988) - Educational console, wireless keyboard

### Motion/kinesthetic subagent
- Sega R360 (1990) - 360° rotating arcade sphere, 1,100 kg
- Sega Galaxy Force Super Deluxe (1988) - 335° rotating cockpit
- Tatsumi TX-1 (1983) - First rotating cockpit + force feedback + triple screen

### Optical/light subagent
- Sega Activator (1992) - Octagonal IR beam-breaking ring → DISQUALIFIED: actual release Nov 1993
- Asaf Gurner's Light Harp (1988-1991) - Parent of Activator
- First Optical Mice (1980) - Borderline fit

### Musical HCI subagent
- VideoHarp (1989) - CMU camera-based multi-touch
- Very Nervous System (1982-1991) - ALREADY DEFERRED (agent missed)
- Akai EWI 1000 (1987) - Capacitive touch wind controller, CC images

## Phase 2: Curation review

### Selected for promotion:
1. **Sega R360 (1990)** - Extreme whole-body kinesthetic HCI. 1,100 kg sphere does full 360° barrel rolls with player strapped inside. ~100 units, ~$90K. Peak embodiment. Distinct from Hard Drivin' and Hang-On.
2. **Akai EWI 1000 (1987)** - Capacitive touch wind controller. Zero moving keys. Breath + bite + capacitive finger sensing. First mass-market capacitive touch instrument, ~20 years before touchscreen phones. Wikimedia CC images.

### Disqualified:
- Activator: 1993 release (outside 1992 era window)
- Very Nervous System: Already deferred
- Intellivoice: Speak & Spell covers speech synthesis; output-only
- Terebikko: HCI is just 4 colored buttons
- VTech Socrates: Standard input paradigms, just kid-sized
- The Robotic Workshop / Fischertechnik: Overlap with AtariLab
- Galaxy Force: Midpoint between Hang-On and R360
- VideoHarp: Academic prototype, paywalled images
- Light Harp: Patent ancestor of Activator
- Optical Mice: Too foundational, no specific notable artifact

### Considered but not selected this run:
- Tatsumi TX-1 (1983): Triple first (rotating cockpit, force feedback, triple screen). Strong but overlapping with Hard Drivin' (force feedback) and R360 (rotating cockpit). Fair use images only.

## What Changed

**2 new artifacts promoted to the HCI Museum collection:**

1. **Mattel Synsonics Drums (1981)** — Mattel Electronics' toy drum synthesizer with a hybrid manual/automatic interface: playable with drumsticks on four velocity-sensitive rubber pads, programmable with auto-repeat buttons, or both layered simultaneously. The bass drum has no pad — it exists only as programmed patterns. Kraftwerk adopted it professionally. 1 CC BY 2.0 image.

2. **AEA PK-232 Pakratt 232 (1986)** — Multimode amateur radio data controller with a 21-LED front panel dashboard. Sits between a computer and two radios, decoding packet radio, RTTY, Morse, weather fax, and more from the radio spectrum. The physical ritual of tuning a radio while watching LEDs flicker and text emerge from static made the invisible spectrum feel like a data network. 1 CC BY-SA 3.0 image (PK-232MBX ~1991 variant shown, same form factor as 1986 original).

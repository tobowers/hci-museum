## 14:10 UTC — Candidate shortlisting
Shortlisted strongest for deep research:
1. **CMU Sensor Frame (1985)** — Pinch-to-zoom origin. Multi-touch with finger-angle sensing. Steve Jobs visited. Paul McAvinney.
2. **Sega Hang-On (1985)** — Full-body leaning motorcycle arcade. Yu Suzuki. Launched taikan movement. ~20K units.
3. **Konix Multisystem (1988-89)** — Unreleased force-feedback console-in-controller. Modular attachments. UK design.
4. **Miracle Piano Teaching System (1990)** — Real piano keyboard as NES/SNES/Genesis controller. Educational gaming bridge.
5. **UPIC (1977)** — Xenakis' drawing-to-sound tablet. Rethinking musical composition as graphical act.

## Phase 2: Subagent results review

All 6 subagents returned. Strong candidates identified:

### Top picks for promotion:
1. **Canon Cat (1987)** — Jef Raskin's radical text-centric computer. LEAP keys. No files/apps/OS. $1,495, ~20K units. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
2. **VersaBraille (1979)** — First US refreshable Braille display. TSI. 20-cell piezoelectric. Tactile output — genuinely new modality. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
3. **Puma RS Computer Tennis Shoes (1985)** — Foot-worn pedometer+computer with Apple IIe/C64 serial download. $200. Weird, commercial, memorable. ⭐⭐⭐⭐

- Talk 'n Play (1984/85): Cassette interactive, overlaps with 2-XL.
- Questron Wand (1984): Conductive ink wand. Collector blog images only.

### Analog/Physical-to-Digital (subagent 7)

- Micomsoft Sensor Kid (1988): MSX multi-sensor. Similar to AtariLab.
- Soundbeam: Already in museum.
- KoalaPad: Already in museum.

- WorldKey Kiosks (1982): Great story, no institutional images.
- AgVision (1982): Blue TRS-80 for farmers. Videotex, low visual appeal.

## Phase 2: Candidates Selected

Three candidates selected for promotion:
1. **Minsky Sandpaper (1990)** — computational haptics origin, 2-DOF texture-synthesizing joystick. MIT Media Lab/UNC. Rich sources, good images from mminsky.com and KCL.
2. **Quantel Paintbox (1981)** — $250K broadcast graphics workstation with first pressure-sensitive stylus. Wikimedia Commons images, excellent press coverage.
3. **Tongue Touch Keypad (1991)** — intraoral dental retainer with 9-key keypad for quadriplegic computer control. FDA-cleared, well-sourced via SBIR/patents. Patent drawings only (device photos unavailable — small production, custom-fitted, intraoral).

Not promoted from this run:
- **Aesthedes (1984)** — incredible 6-screen design workstation. 2 working units at HomeComputerMuseum. Defer for future run.
- **Hotz MIDI Translator (1990)** — radical reconfigurable touch-pad music controller. Interesting but overlaps with existing music HCI entries.
- **Laser Harp (1980)** — optical beam interruption instrument. More art installation than HCI artifact.
- **Datasoft Le Stick (1981)** — mercury tilt joystick. Fun but simple tilt interaction.

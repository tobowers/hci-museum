## Yeoman Plotter notes
- Yeoman Navigator (1985): paper chart navigation with 4-LED guided-fix mouse. The interface guides the human's HAND to the right position on the chart by directional arrows. Unique interaction: the human becomes a servo in a visual-motor loop, following light signals until all four arrows extinguish.
- Images verified on Commons: "Yacht_chart_table.jpg" (CC BY-SA 3.0) and "Yeoman_jpg200.jpg" (PD)
- Not in data.ts, not in wiki — ready to promote.
